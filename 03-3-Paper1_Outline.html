According to the grading rubric, a convincing argument paper MUST include critical analysis!

  • There are a ton of ways to add critical analysis to a paper, but for our exercise I will ask each of you to include a counter-argument paragraph

  • By that I mean, an alternative answer to the question that you present IN ITS STRONGEST FORM, and then address

  • e.g. explain why this strong answer doesn’t replace your answer


Your paper offers three premises or reasons your chosen event is the most important

  • A compelling counter-argument should use those exact same criteria!

  • If you tell the reader that economic impacts make your event most important, then other events with large economic impacts must also be important

  • If you tell the reader threats to human life are important, then other events with large death counts are also important

  • Make sense?


So, pairs, your final job today is to help each other brainstorm a counter-argument for each others’ papers

  • I am asking you to come up with an alternative “most important event” that would make a good counter for your specific paper
